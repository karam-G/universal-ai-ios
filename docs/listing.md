# ALLIN — App Store Listing Draft

**Name:** ALLIN Daily  
**Subtitle:** Daily discovery & tools  
**Primary category:** Lifestyle (confirm after final product scope)  
**Price:** Free

## Description

Make a little space for discovery every day.

ALLIN brings together quick self-discovery, a fresh daily word challenge, personal prompts, and useful everyday tools in one focused iPhone experience.

**Discover**
Explore your identity with a short personality-style quiz and keep your result on your device.

**Daily**
Take on a five-letter word puzzle with six attempts. Track your activity and build a simple daily streak.

**Connect**
Reflect on a daily prompt and save your own responses and notes privately on your device.

**Tools**
Use a text and word counter, percentage calculator, and decision picker without an account or subscription.

**Your privacy**
ALLIN is local-first. Your profile, quiz result, puzzle progress, votes, and posts stay on your device in this release. There is no account, cloud sync, advertising, or analytics.

## Review notes

- No account or login is required.
- User data is stored locally on device.
- The Connect section is a private/local experience in this release; it is not a public social network.
- The app includes an in-app Privacy & Data screen and a local data reset control.
