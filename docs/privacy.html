Privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026 · Version 0.3.0

ALLIN is currently a local-first iOS app. It does not require an account and does not send your profile, quiz answers, puzzle progress, poll choices, notes, or activity to an ALLIN server. The current release does not include advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Information stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, ALLIN may store a display name and profile preferences, quiz answers and results, daily puzzle answers and streaks, poll selections, locally saved posts or notes, app preferences, and activity counters. This information is used only to operate the app on your device.

Sharing

If you choose a Share action, iOS presents its system share sheet. Content is sent only to the app or service you select, under that provider's own privacy terms. ALLIN does not share content in the background.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The current release does not use third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking technologies. It does not sell personal information.

Data deletion

Use the in-app Reset local data control in Profile to clear ALLIN's locally stored app data. Deleting the app also removes its local app container, subject to device backups or operating-system retention managed by Apple.

Children's privacy

ALLIN does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The current release does not transmit user profile or activity data to ALLIN servers.

Changes

If ALLIN adds accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, or other data processing in a future release, this policy and the app's privacy disclosures will be updated before those features are enabled.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the project support page. Please do not post passwords, private identifiers, or sensitive personal information in public issues.
