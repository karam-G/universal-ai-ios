How can we help?

For help with ALLIN, to report a bug, or to request a feature, open an issue in the project's support tracker.

Report a problem

Include your ALLIN app version, iOS version, and the steps needed to reproduce the problem. Do not include passwords, private identifiers, or sensitive personal information.

Open ALLIN support

Current release: 0.3.0. ALLIN currently stores app data locally on your device and does not sync it to an ALLIN server.
